Bog Blitz support: support@cloudshadowstudios.com

Bog Blitz by Cloudshadow Studios is a retro obstacle run across mountain biking, whitewater kayaking and wingsuit flying. When writing, include your iPhone model, iOS version, the level you were playing and a short description of what happened. A screen recording helps a lot.

I bought Pro but it isn’t unlocked

On the title screen tap Go Pro, then Restore purchase. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, works on all your devices and supports Family Sharing. For refunds, use Apple purchase support.

My progress is missing on a new iPhone

Progress is saved on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account. Sign in with the same Apple Account, turn on iCloud, then open Bog Blitz and give it a moment to sync. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

The controls

Bike: tap Jump to hop (hold it for a bigger jump) and hold Duck to get low. Kayak: steer with the stick and flick it forward for a power stroke. Wingsuit: fly with the stick and tap the other side of the screen to flare. You can move the buttons, swap the stick side and change their size in Settings.

A level is too hard

Open Settings and lower Game speed to 85% or 70%. If you wipe out a lot on one level, the game offers a slower speed too. You can switch it back any time.

Goals, secret levels and Mirror mode

Every level has three goals: finish without a wipeout, collect 80% of the acorns, and find the golden acorn. Earn all three goals on every level of a sport to open its secret special level. Mirror mode opens once you reach 100%. Clear all 27 main levels to unlock the golden acorn finder in Settings.

Hard mode

Hard mode is part of Pro. Every level runs 12% faster with only 2 lives. Turn it on or off in Settings or from Challenges.

Widgets and Live Activities

Bog Blitz has three Home Screen widgets: Daily Trail (today’s course, your streak and a countdown to the next trail), Trail Map (where you are on the mountain) and Acorn Drop (tap to catch an acorn once an hour). To add one, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Bog Blitz. Acorns you catch go into your Trail Shop savings the next time you open the game.

After a Daily Trail, a Live Activity counts down to tomorrow’s trail on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Endless Blitz runs and time trials show your distance or your gap to your ghost there too. You can turn Live Activities off in your iPhone’s Settings under Bog Blitz.

Game Center

Achievements and leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then open Challenges in Bog Blitz and tap Game Center.
