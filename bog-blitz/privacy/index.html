Bog Blitz privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Bog Blitz is made by Cloudshadow Studios. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@cloudshadowstudios.com.

What Bog Blitz stores

The game stores your progress so you can pick up where you left off: levels cleared, goals earned, acorn totals, best times, your chosen rider and gear, and your settings. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

The game includes no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and there is no Bog Blitz account. We do not collect, sell or share your information.

Where data is stored

Your progress is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, a copy syncs through your own Apple Account using iCloud so it appears on your other devices. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, not by Cloudshadow Studios, and we can’t see it. Apple processes iCloud information under its privacy policy.

Bog Blitz’s Home Screen widgets and Live Activities read your progress from your iPhone to show it. That information stays on your device.

Game Center

If you’re signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple’s Game Center service so they can be shown to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players. Game Center is provided by Apple under its privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings.

Purchases

Bog Blitz Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game that Pro is unlocked. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never seen or stored by Bog Blitz.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your progress by deleting the app; if iCloud sync is on, also remove Bog Blitz’s data from your iCloud storage settings. If you email support, our email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these only to respond and troubleshoot, and you can ask us to delete that correspondence. Rights available to you depend on where you live.

Children’s information

Bog Blitz is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

This website

Our policy and support pages use GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its privacy statement. Email links open your chosen email app.

Changes

We update this page and its effective date if our data practices change. For questions, contact support@cloudshadowstudios.com.
