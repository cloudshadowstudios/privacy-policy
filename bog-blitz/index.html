Bog Blitz

Ride. Paddle. Fly. A retro pixel obstacle run across three outdoor sports.

Mountain bike the trails, kayak the rapids, then leave the waterfall in a wingsuit. 68 hand-made levels, the Deep Caverns, Mirror and Hard modes, a new Daily Trail every day, and a Trail Shop full of gear.

For iPhone. Free to play: the first five levels of every sport and the Daily Trail. Bog Blitz Pro is one purchase that unlocks everything else. No ads, no subscriptions.
