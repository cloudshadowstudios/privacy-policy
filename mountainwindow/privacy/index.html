Mountain Window privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Mountain Window: Trail Weather (“Mountain Window”) is made by Cloudshadow Studios. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@cloudshadowstudios.com.

The short version: Mountain Window has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. To get you a forecast, it has to send the coordinates of the place you’re looking at to the weather services listed below. Cloudshadow Studios doesn’t run a server that receives your locations, and we don’t collect, sell or share your information.

What stays on your devices

Your saved places, planned outings, high points, launch directions you enter, settings and recently downloaded forecasts are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and shared with Mountain Window’s widgets through storage on the same device. Downloaded forecasts are deleted automatically after about a week. If you use Near me, the app keeps your most recent position on the device so it can show the nearest recent forecast when you’re moving or offline. None of this is sent to us.

Location

Location access is optional. If you allow it while using the app, Mountain Window uses your position to show weather and spots near you. Mountain Window asks for access “Always” only if you turn on “Offer to start when I arrive” for an outing; it then checks on your device when you reach the place you planned. You can change location access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mountain Window.

What is sent to weather and map services

To show a forecast, Mountain Window sends the latitude and longitude of the place you’re viewing (your current position when you use Near me, or a place you searched for or saved) directly from your device to:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), for forecasts, the forecast at a high point’s elevation, terrain heights and air quality. Open-Meteo says its server logs can contain the coordinates requested and are deleted after 90 days, and that it doesn’t link them to anyone’s identity. See Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information.
  • Apple WeatherKit, for official weather alerts and next-hour precipitation.
  • Apple Maps, for the map, place search and nearby places. Apple handles WeatherKit and Maps requests under Apple’s privacy policy.

The map’s trails, trailheads, campsites, crags and launch sites are downloaded as files covering areas of roughly 25 km (about 15 miles) across, from spots.cloudshadowstudios.com. Those files are served by Cloudflare. Downloading one reveals the general area you’re looking at and, like any internet request, your IP address. We don’t keep logs of who downloads them.

These requests don’t include your name, Apple Account, contacts or any identifier from us. Like any internet request, they include your device’s IP address, which these services need in order to send the forecast back.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, departure reminders and trip weather alerts are scheduled and sent by your device. To check for trip weather changes in the background, the app fetches the forecast for your upcoming outings from the services above. No notification content is sent to us.

Apple Watch and widgets

Your iPhone sends your places, outings and forecasts directly to your Apple Watch so the watch app and its complications stay up to date. The Apple Watch app can also request forecasts from the services above itself. Widgets read the forecasts already saved on your device.

Sharing

If you share a forecast, a trip or a calendar entry, Mountain Window creates it on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so it only goes where you choose to send it. A shared trip is a small file with the place, activity, time, route and extra gear, plus the meeting point if you choose to include it. Private notes, packing progress, travel time and reminders are never included.

Tips

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. They’re bought through the App Store; Apple processes the payment and only tells the app that the purchase completed. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Mountain Window contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage totals, which don’t identify you.

Your choices

  • Delete saved places and outings in the app.
  • Turn off Location Services or notifications for Mountain Window in iPhone Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

If you email support, our email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these only to respond and troubleshoot, and you can ask us to delete that correspondence. Rights available to you depend on where you live.

Children’s information

Mountain Window isn’t directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information on our servers, we don’t knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we’ll also mention it in the app’s release notes.
