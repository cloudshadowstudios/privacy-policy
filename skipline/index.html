Skipline support: support@cloudshadowstudios.com

Skipline by Cloudshadow Studios is an ad-free remote, launcher and set of widgets for Roku TVs. Tap an app on your iPhone and your TV turns on and opens it, without the TV’s ad-filled home screen. When writing, include your iPhone model, your TV’s brand and model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Which TVs does Skipline work with?

Roku TVs (like onn, TCL and Hisense Roku TVs) and Roku streaming sticks and boxes. The sleep timer and HDMI inputs are on Roku TVs only, since sticks and boxes don’t have them. Your iPhone and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Skipline can’t find my TV

Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network; guest networks usually block this. Check that iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Skipline is turned on, and that the TV is plugged in. You can also enter the TV’s address by hand: on the TV, go to Settings → Network → About.

Skipline says my TV is blocking phone control

On the TV, go to Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps, and choose Enabled (not Limited). On older TVs it’s Network access → Default.

My TV won’t turn on from my phone

Turn on Fast TV Start on the TV: Settings → System → Power → Fast TV Start. Without it, the TV goes fully to sleep and can’t hear your phone.

The sleep timer landed on the wrong time

Your TV doesn’t tell apps whether a sleep timer is running, so Skipline keeps track of the ones it sets. If you set or cancel a timer with the TV’s own remote, the next one Skipline sets can land one choice off. Set it again from Skipline and it’ll be right from then on.

Why does setting the volume take a few seconds?

TVs don’t let apps jump straight to a number, so Skipline turns the volume all the way down and then back up to the level you picked.

Widgets, Control Center and the Lock Screen

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Skipline. There are Channels, Remote, Now Playing, Sleep Timer and Scenes widgets. For Control Center, open Control Center, tap + and search for Skipline. The Lock Screen remote appears on its own when you open a channel or set a sleep timer from Skipline, and goes away when you turn the TV off. You can turn Live Activities off in your iPhone’s Settings under Skipline.

Scenes

A scene does a few things at once. Movie night turns the TV on and asks which app to open, Bedtime sets a 30-minute sleep timer and turns the volume down, and Baby’s asleep drops the volume to a quiet level. Tap the pencil on a scene in the Scenes tab to change what it does, or tap + to build your own.

Sharing the TV with your household

In Skipline, tap ••• → Share this TV and have them scan the QR code with their iPhone camera. They don’t need any passwords: your streaming apps stay signed in on the TV.

Siri

Try “Open Netflix with Skipline”, “Switch the TV to HDMI 3 with Skipline”, “Turn the volume to 20 with Skipline”, “Set a sleep timer for 1 hour with Skipline” or “Movie night with Skipline”.
