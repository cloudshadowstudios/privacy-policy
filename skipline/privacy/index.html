Skipline privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Skipline is made by Cloudshadow Studios. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@cloudshadowstudios.com.

The short version

Skipline has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It talks only to the TV on your own Wi-Fi, and nothing about you or what you watch is sent to us or anyone else. Skipline never asks for or stores your streaming passwords; those stay signed in on your TV.

How Skipline works

Your TV has a built-in control service on your home Wi-Fi. Skipline finds your TV on your network and sends it commands directly, like “open Netflix” or “volume up”. These commands go from your iPhone to your TV over your own network. They never pass through our servers; we don’t have any.

To find your TV, Skipline asks for iPhone’s Local Network permission. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, but Skipline can’t reach your TV without it.

What Skipline stores

On your iPhone only, so the app and its widgets work: your TV’s name, its address on your home network and its serial number (so Skipline can find it again if your router gives it a new address); the list of apps and inputs on your TV, their icons, your channel order and the ones you’ve hidden; and your scenes, settings and when a sleep timer you set will end.

Skipline also asks your TV what’s on screen and whether it’s on, to show it in the app, its widgets and on your Lock Screen. That information stays on your iPhone. Skipline does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Sharing your TV

If you use Share this TV, Skipline makes an invite link and QR code containing your TV’s name and home-network address, and your channel layout if you choose to include it. It goes only to the people you send it to, contains no passwords, and doesn’t work outside your home Wi-Fi.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri with Skipline, Apple handles your voice request under its privacy policy. Skipline only receives the action you asked for, such as which channel to open.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Skipline includes no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share your information. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports, which contain no personal information.

Your choices

Settings → Switch to a different TV removes your TV and its channel list from Skipline, and deleting the app removes everything it stored on that iPhone. If you email support, our email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these only to respond and troubleshoot, and you can ask us to delete that correspondence. Rights available to you depend on where you live.

Children’s information

Skipline is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

This website

Our policy and support pages use GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its privacy statement. Email links open your chosen email app.
