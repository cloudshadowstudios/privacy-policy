Apps

Both are on the App Store now.

Habitcrawl

Most habit trackers give you a checkmark. Habitcrawl gives you a sword. Every habit you finish becomes a card, and each night your hero takes on one floor of the dungeon with the hand you built that day. Seven floors, one boss, every week.

For iPhone and Apple Watch. Works with Apple Health, Siri and Home Screen widgets. No ads, no account.

Little Relay

A calm care journal for parents and caregivers, from pregnancy through toddlerhood. Log feeds, sleep and diapers with one thumb, see the patterns, and hand off care to a partner without the texts.

For iPhone and Apple Watch. Free to use, with a Family plan for shared care.

Mountain Window

Coming soon

Find the best weather window for your next outing. Pick a place and an activity, like hiking, mountain biking, camping or paragliding, and Mountain Window ranks the hours ahead by rain, wind, temperature and daylight. It also tells you when to leave and what to pack.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Home Screen widgets. Mountain forecasts include freezing level, summit wind and storm potential.
