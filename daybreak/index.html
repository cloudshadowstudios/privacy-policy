Common questions

How do I restore Summit Pass on a new phone?

Open Daybreak → Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My fasts didn't show up on my new phone

Check that Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud is on in Daybreak on both devices, and that both are signed in to the same iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes on Wi‑Fi.

The widget or Live Activity isn't updating

Open Daybreak once. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours, and opening the app starts a new one. Also check iPhone Settings → Daybreak → Live Activities is on.

The Apple Watch app isn't on my watch

On your iPhone, open the Watch app → My Watch, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Daybreak.

How do I stop Daybreak reading Apple Health?

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Daybreak, and turn off what you don't want to share.
