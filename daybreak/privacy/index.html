Daybreak Privacy Policy

Daybreak: Fasting Timer · Effective November 3, 2026

The short version
  • Daybreak has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account (iCloud sync can be turned off).
  • Cloudshadow Studios never receives, sees, sells or shares your data.

Who we are

Daybreak: Fasting Timer ("Daybreak") is made by Cloudshadow Studios. If you have a question about this policy, email support@cloudshadowstudios.com.

What Daybreak stores

To work, Daybreak keeps the following on your iPhone and Apple Watch:

  • Your fasts: start and end times, the plan you chose and whether you reached your goal.
  • Water you log in Daybreak, and your daily water goal.
  • Your settings, such as your chosen plan and notification preference.

We don't operate servers that collect this information, and we have no way to see it.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Daybreak asks for permission to:

  • Save the water and weight you log in Daybreak to Apple Health.
  • Read your sleep, workouts, weight and blood glucose, so Daybreak can show how much of a fast you slept through, workouts you did while fasting, your weight trend and your blood sugar during a fast.

Information read from Apple Health is shown to you on your device only. Daybreak does not store it, does not copy it to iCloud, and never sends it to us or anyone else. It is never used for advertising or sold. You can change or remove Daybreak's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off in Daybreak's Settings. When it's on, your fasts, the water you log in Daybreak, your plan and your water goal are kept in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices and come back if you get a new phone. This data belongs to your Apple Account; Cloudshadow Studios can't access it. Data read from Apple Health is never part of iCloud sync. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Watch

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange your fast and water information directly with each other so both stay up to date.

Purchases

The optional Summit Pass is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple only tells the app whether the purchase is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Daybreak schedules reminders, such as when you reach your fasting goal, on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Daybreak contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports, which contain no personal information.

Children

Daybreak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Fasting isn't right for everyone; anyone under 18 should talk to a doctor before fasting.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete individual fasts in Daybreak's Journal by swiping left.
  • Turn off iCloud sync in Daybreak → Settings.
  • Delete Daybreak's iCloud data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage) → Daybreak.
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stored on that device.
  • Remove Apple Health access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Daybreak.

Not medical advice

Daybreak is a timer and journal. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to your doctor before starting a fasting routine, especially if you're pregnant, managing a medical condition or have a history of disordered eating.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
