What Habitcrawl is

Habitcrawl is a habit tracker with a game built around it. You choose the habits, and you are responsible for what you track and how you act on it. The app is not medical, fitness or mental health advice. Talk to a professional before changing your exercise, sleep, diet or medication.

If you connect Apple Health, Habitcrawl uses it only to check off the habits you linked. You can turn that off at any time in the Health app.

Habitcrawl Pro

Habitcrawl is free to use. Habitcrawl Pro is optional and is sold either as a subscription or as Lifetime Pro, a one-time purchase that never renews.

The purchase screen and Apple’s confirmation sheet show the price in your currency, the billing period, what’s included and any free trial you’re eligible for. Apple charges your Apple Account.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.
  • A free trial turns into the paid plan shown unless you cancel before it ends.
  • To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Habitcrawl Pro.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

To get Pro back on a new phone, use Settings › Restore Purchases in Habitcrawl with the Apple Account that bought it. Apple handles billing and refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If a subscription ends, your habits, history and hero stay on your device. Features that need Pro stop until you subscribe again.

Your data

Your habits and progress are stored on your device. iCloud backup, a Pro feature, keeps a copy in your own private iCloud, which depends on Apple’s iCloud service and your connection. We can’t see or recover your data, so keep iCloud backup on if you want to move to a new phone. The privacy policy has the details.
