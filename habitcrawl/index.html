Habitcrawl

Most habit trackers give you a checkmark. Habitcrawl gives you a sword.

Every habit you complete becomes a card. Each night your hero descends one floor of the dungeon and fights with the hand you built that day. Seven floors, one boss, every week.

For iPhone and Apple Watch. Works with Apple Health, Siri and Home Screen widgets. No ads, no account.
